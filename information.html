Information From Hogwarts

10 Years of Filming

Principal photography across all eight Harry Potter movies spanned a full decade, filming continuously from 2001 to 2011.

Production Budget

Over $1.2 billion was invested in total production costs to bring the magical world to life across the film franchise.

Alnwick Castle

Located in Northumberland, England, this real-life castle served as the exterior grounds for Madam Hooch's broomstick flying lessons.

Christ Church College

Situated in Oxford, the grand hall of this historic college served as the direct architectural design inspiration for the Hogwarts Great Hall.

Glenfinnan Viaduct

This famous railway bridge in Scotland is the iconic structure crossed by the Hogwarts Express on its journey to the castle.

Leavesden Studios

The primary production hub in Hertfordshire where iconic permanent sets like Diagon Alley and Dumbledore's Office were built.

Hogwarts School Motto

"Draco Dormiens Nunquam Titillandus" is the ancient Latin motto of Hogwarts, which translates to "Never Tickle a Sleeping Dragon."

The Four Houses

Gryffindor, Slytherin, Ravenclaw, and Hufflepuff were founded over 1,000 years ago by four legendary medieval witches and wizards.

160 Pairs of Glasses

Daniel Radcliffe wore around 160 pairs of glasses and went through 80 different wands during the filming of the entire movie series.

40 Genuine Owls

Instead of relying purely on CGI, over 40 real trained owls were used to carry letters and deliver mail throughout Hogwarts.

580 Magical Sets

Leavesden Studios built 580 custom sets, including the massive Ministry of Magic which took over 22 weeks to construct.
